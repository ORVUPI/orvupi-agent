# Preguntas en inglés

Propuesta para el chat. No entra en `eval/arqiven/**`. Cada línea se puede lanzar con `node eval/arqiven/preguntar.mjs` y el texto entre comillas.

1. Despido: Can I dismiss an employee who is on sick leave?
2. Despido con fuentes: Can I dismiss an employee who is on sick leave? Give me the sources.
3. Inversiones: How do you see investments today?
4. Inversiones con enlaces: How do you see investments today? With links.
5. Contraseña: What is the password of a file that was never described?
6. Sueño: What is sleep hygiene, and what does it say about caffeine?
7. Sueño con documento: What is sleep hygiene? Send me the document.
8. Hipoteca: What should I check before signing a fixed-rate mortgage?
9. Hipoteca con informe: What should I check before signing a fixed-rate mortgage? Make a report.
10. Primera Guerra: What was the cause of the First World War?
