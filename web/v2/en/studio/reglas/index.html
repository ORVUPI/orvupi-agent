Create with ORVUPI.

Choose a template, enter a name, title and body, select a background and octopus or your own logo, choose a format and download the PNG. Sharing opens the system menu; it does not post automatically.

Your data stays here

The logo is read in this browser and is not sent to a server. It is not saved with the draft. The ORVUPI name activates our mascot; another name lets you use your logo without the octopus. Previous drafts are preserved.

Content and limits

Create your own content. The editor retains filters against impersonation and promises of price or returns. An image does not certify an audit or make a token official.
