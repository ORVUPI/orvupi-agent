Security

Check the full address, the source of every fact and the reader’s limits. Never share seed phrases or private keys. ORVUPI does not sign or send transactions.

Official channels

Only the channels on this list are official. If an account, website or address is not listed here, treat it as unofficial until you check it on this website.

  • X: x.com/ORVUPI
  • Telegram: t.me/orvupi
  • TikTok: tiktok.com/@orvupi
  • Email: orvupi.hq@gmail.com

There will be fake accounts, websites and token addresses using the ORVUPI name. Any other X account is not an official channel of the brand.

Anti-scam warnings

  • Nobody from ORVUPI will write to you by private message. If it happens, it is a scam: do not reply, do not open links and do not sign anything.
  • A direct message offering a presale, whitelist, airdrop, refund, investment or “help to mint” is a scam.
  • The only official ORVUPI address is on orvupi.com/token: any token claiming to be ORVUPI is not official.
  • We never ask for money, funds, private keys or seed phrases by email or direct message.
  • Never share your private key, seed phrase or access codes.
  • Do not connect your wallet on websites that are not on this list.
  • Nobody official will promise you returns, a price, a listing or a public sale.
  • This website does not sign, does not hold funds and does not send transactions: it has no wallet or keys.
  • It is not an autonomous agent: it does not sign, hold funds or trade.
