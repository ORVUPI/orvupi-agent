CurvaCurve

Si la cuenta derivada es del programa de la curva, la ficha lo dice. El avance clásico es un cálculo inferido con la cantidad real inicial pública documentada de la curva (Pump.fun lo llama ‘reserves’) (793100000000000), truncado a 2 decimales hacia cero. No es un campo de la cuenta. Si no hay curva, el avance queda en no aplica y no se rellenan con cero las cantidades de la curva.If the derived account belongs to the curve program, the card says so. Classic progress is an inferred calculation using the documented public initial real curve amount (Pump.fun calls these ‘reserves’) (793100000000000), truncated to 2 decimals toward zero. It is not a field of the account. If there is no curve, progress stays not applicable and the curve amounts are not filled in with zero.
